Privacy Policy

At Campus Connect, we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is handled when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Campus Connect may collect information that users voluntarily provide when using the platform, including:

  • Full name.
  • Email address.
  • Student information.
  • Login details provided during registration.
  • Information submitted through website forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided by users may be used to:

  • Support the website's features.
  • Improve the student experience.
  • Help users access their accounts.
  • Provide relevant campus information.
  • Improve the functionality of the platform.

3. Information Protection

We encourage users to protect their personal information and avoid sharing sensitive details with unauthorized individuals.

Campus Connect is currently a frontend project. It does not yet have a backend security system or centralized database for storing user accounts.

4. Information Sharing

Campus Connect does not intentionally sell users' personal information.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into public sections of the website.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Campus Connect may use browser storage, including localStorage, to remember certain preferences or information entered on the website.

Information stored in your browser may remain there until it is manually cleared or removed by the website.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Users should have control over their personal information. Depending on applicable privacy laws, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Ask questions about how their information is handled.

7. Children's Privacy

Campus Connect is designed primarily for students. We do not intentionally encourage children to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information.

8. Third-Party Websites

Campus Connect may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party websites.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Campus Connect may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions, suggestions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the Campus Connect development team.
